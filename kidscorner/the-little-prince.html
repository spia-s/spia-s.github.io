The Little Prince

Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

French (Original) 1943 Ages 8-99 Fiction Philosophical / Illustrated Novel
Languages Available: English, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese
KidsCorner Recommendation ★★★★★

Why Read This Book?

A timeless classic beloved across all ages and cultures, available in virtually every language; perfect for multilingual collections and cross-cultural literary exploration.

Book Summary

A pilot stranded in the Sahara meets a mysterious little prince who has traveled from a tiny asteroid. Through their encounters, the story explores themes of love, loss, and the importance of seeing with the heart rather than the eyes.

About the Author

Antoine de Saint-Exupéry (1900-1944) was a French writer, poet, and aviator. His experiences as a pilot deeply influenced his writing, and The Little Prince is his most famous work, written during his exile in the United States during World War II.
