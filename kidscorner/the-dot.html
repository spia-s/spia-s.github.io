The Dot

Peter H. Reynolds

English (Original) 2003 Ages 4-8 Fiction Picture Book
Languages Available: English, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese
KidsCorner Recommendation ★★★★★

Why Read This Book?

Celebrated on International Dot Day (Sept 15); a powerful message about creativity and self-belief for young children; used widely in schools worldwide.

Book Summary

Vashti believes she cannot draw, but her teacher encourages her to just make a mark and see where it takes her. A single dot becomes the start of an artistic journey and newfound confidence.
