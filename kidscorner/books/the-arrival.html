The Arrival

Shaun Tan

English (Original) 2006 Ages 10-99 Fiction Graphic Novel / Wordless Picture Book
Languages Available: English, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese
KidsCorner Recommendation ★★★★★

Why Read This Book?

Unique wordless format transcends language barriers; powerful tool for discussing immigration and empathy; multiple award winner; ideal for multilingual classrooms.

Book Summary

A wordless graphic novel following an immigrant who leaves his family behind and travels to a strange new country. Told entirely through sepia-toned illustrations, it captures the disorientation and wonder of starting over in an unfamiliar world.

About the Author

Shaun Tan is an Australian artist, writer, and filmmaker known for his illustrated books that deal with social, historical, and political themes. The Arrival is considered his masterpiece.
