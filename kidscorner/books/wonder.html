Wonder

R.J. Palacio

English (Original) 2012 Ages 8-14 Fiction Contemporary / Middle Grade Novel
Languages Available: English, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese
KidsCorner Recommendation ★★★★★

Why Read This Book?

Widely used in schools globally to promote empathy and anti-bullying; winner of multiple awards and adapted into a major film; available in many languages.

Book Summary

Ten-year-old Auggie Pullman was born with a facial difference and enters fifth grade for the first time. Told from multiple perspectives, the novel follows his journey to find acceptance and make friends in a new school.

About the Author

R.J. Palacio is a pseudonym for Raquel Jaramillo, a graphic designer and book jacket designer. Wonder was her debut novel, inspired by a real-life encounter with a child with a facial difference.
