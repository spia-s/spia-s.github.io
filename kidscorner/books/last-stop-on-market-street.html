Last Stop on Market Street

Matt de la Peña

English (Original) 2015 Ages 4-8 Fiction Picture Book
Languages Available: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French
KidsCorner Recommendation ★★★★★

Why Read This Book?

Newbery Medal winner; Browses empathy, gratitude and community in an urban setting; represents diversity authentically; widely used in social-emotional learning curricula.

Book Summary

After church, CJ and his grandmother ride the bus across town. CJ wonders why they don't have certain things others seem to have, but his grandmother shows him the beauty in their community and the importance of giving back.

Themes

Community Gratitude Empathy Social Differences Service Urban Life Family

Awards & Recognition

  • Newbery Medal (2016)
  • Caldecott Honor (2016)
  • Pura Belpré Award (2016)

About the Author

Matt de la Peña is an American author of young adult fiction and picture books. He won the Newbery Medal for Last Stop on Market Street.
