The Very Hungry Caterpillar

Eric Carle

English (Original) 1969 Ages 2-6 Fiction Picture Book
Languages Available: English, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese
KidsCorner Recommendation ★★★★★

Why Read This Book?

One of the best-selling picture books of all time; available in bilingual editions; perfect for early childhood literacy and language learning across cultures.

Book Summary

A hungry caterpillar eats through a variety of foods during the week before forming a cocoon and emerging as a beautiful butterfly. A simple story introducing counting, days of the week, and the concept of metamorphosis.

About the Author

Eric Carle (1929-2021) was an American author, designer, and illustrator of children's books. His distinctive collage technique and bold colors have made his books beloved by generations of children worldwide.
