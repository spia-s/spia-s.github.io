The Hobbit

J.R.R. Tolkien

English (Original) 1937 Ages 10-99 Fiction Fantasy / Adventure Novel
Languages Available: English, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese
KidsCorner Recommendation ★★★★★

Why Read This Book?

One of the most influential fantasy novels ever written; foundational text for the genre; suitable for upper primary and secondary students; widely translated.

Book Summary

Bilbo Baggins, a comfort-loving hobbit, is swept into an epic quest with a wizard and thirteen dwarves to reclaim a treasure guarded by the dragon Smaug, discovering unexpected courage along the way.

About the Author
